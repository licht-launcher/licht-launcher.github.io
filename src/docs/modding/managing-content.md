# Managing installed content

Each profile keeps its own installed content, such as mods, separate from every other profile. From the launcher you can view a profile's installed content without leaving the app.

## What works today

- **Listing** the content installed in a profile.
- **Enabling and disabling** items.
- **Removing** items.
- **Game settings and logs** for the profile, from the launcher itself.

## Not available yet

Installing, updating and resolving dependencies for individual mods from inside the launcher is still in development. It is tracked as item 9.2 on the [roadmap](https://github.com/mateusneiva/licht-launcher/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md).

Until then, [modpacks](/docs/modding/modpacks) are the way to get a complete set of mods into a profile.

::: info Back up your worlds
Profiles hold your worlds and other user files. Back up anything you care about before removing content or deleting a profile.
:::
